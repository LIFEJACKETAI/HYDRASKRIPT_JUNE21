## Do not make a Stripe “$0.00/month” product

A $0 monthly Stripe subscription usually creates extra complexity without much benefit:

* More webhook edge cases.  
* More subscription states to manage.  
* Possible tax/billing weirdness.  
* Users may be confused: “Why am I subscribing for free?”  
* It does not protect you from abuse.  
* It adds friction compared to a normal free account.

Instead, treat free users as:

TypeScript

tier \= "free"

inside your own database.

## What your Free plan should include

I would make the Free plan something like this:

### Free / Sandbox Plan

* One-time signup bonus: 20–30 credits.  
* Monthly refill: optional, but very small, maybe 10 credits/month.  
* Max 1 active book.  
* Max 2 adult chapters, or max 3 children’s pages.  
* No full 15-page children’s book generation.  
* No audiobook generation.  
* No paid image fallbacks like Replicate/FAL for free users.  
* Hugging Face image generation only, if available.  
* Watermarked PDF export, or no export unless paid.  
* Low queue priority.  
* Storage retention: 7–30 days.  
* 1 style profile maximum, maybe max 3 samples.  
* Rate limits by user/IP/device.

This gives users a real taste without letting them burn your API budget.

## Why not a normal trial period?

A traditional “14-day trial” works for many SaaS products, but for AI apps it can be risky because users can consume real API resources immediately.

For HydraSkript, a user could sign up, generate images, books, audiobooks, exports, then leave. That costs you money.

If you do offer a trial, make it this type:

### Safer trial option

* 7-day trial of Starter or Author  
* Requires payment method, or at least strong abuse protection.  
* Trial includes a fixed credit cap, for example 100 credits.  
* No unlimited generation.  
* No Studio/Publisher trial unless manually approved.  
* Trial credits do not roll over.  
* One trial per user/payment method/IP/device fingerprint.

But honestly, for your audience, I would lead with the free sandbox instead of a card-required trial.

**AUDIOBOOK PRICING**

This is a classic SaaS pricing dilemma, especially for AI apps where costs are variable and unpredictable.

Given your specific context (\*\*Bootstrap Channel\*\*, \*\*High Cost of TTS\*\*, \*\*Teaching Unit Economics\*\*), here is the definitive strategy:

\#\#\# 🚀 The Winner: \*\*Hybrid Model (Base Allocation \+ Top-Up)\*\*

\*\*Do not make Audiobooks "A-La-Carte Only."\*\*  
\*\*Do not make them "Fully Included" in low tiers.\*\*

Instead, treat Audiobooks as a \*\*"Premium Feature"\*\* that consumes credits at a \*\*much higher rate\*\*, forcing users to either:  
1\.  Be on a high enough tier to afford it from their monthly allowance.  
2\.  Buy an "A-La-Carte" top-up pack if they are on a lower tier but really want audio.

\---

\#\#\# 💡 Why This is the Best Approach

\#\#\#\# 1\. Psychological Value vs. Technical Cost  
\*   \*\*The Problem:\*\* If you hide Audiobooks behind a separate "Buy Now" wall \*every single time\*, it feels transactional and annoying. Users think: \*"I already paid $29/month, why do I have to pay again for this button?"\*  
\*   \*\*The Solution:\*\* Keep it inside the main workflow. When they click "Generate Audiobook," the system checks: \*"Do you have 500 credits?"\*  
    \*   If \*\*Yes\*\*: It deducts them instantly. Smooth UX.  
    \*   If \*\*No\*\*: It triggers a modal: \*"You need 450 more credits. Buy a 'Audio Booster Pack' ($15) or upgrade to Publisher Tier."\*

\#\#\#\# 2\. Teaching "Variable Cost" Architecture  
For your YouTube audience, this is a goldmine lesson. You can explain:  
\*   \*"Text generation is cheap and predictable (fixed cost). Audio is expensive and variable (duration-based cost)."\*  
\*   \*"We don't ban the feature; we just price it according to its resource consumption."\*  
\*   This teaches them how to build scalable systems where heavy features don't bankrupt the company.

\#\#\#\# 3\. Flexibility for the User  
\*   \*\*Scenario A:\*\* A user on the \*\*Author Plan ($79/mo)\*\* gets 2,000 credits. They might generate 1 audiobook a month using their included credits. They feel like a king.  
\*   \*\*Scenario B:\*\* A user on the \*\*Starter Plan (Free/$0)\*\* gets 10 credits. They see the Audiobook option. They realize they can't afford it \*yet\*. They buy a \*\*$5 "Mini Pack"\*\* just to try one chapter.  
\*   \*\*Result:\*\* You capture revenue from both the subscriber and the one-off buyer.

\---

\#\#\# 🛠 Implementation Strategy

Here is exactly how to code this logic into your \`HydraSkript\` spec:

\#\#\#\# 1\. Dynamic Credit Pricing (Not Fixed)  
In your \`src/lib/utils/credits.ts\`, do not hardcode "50 credits." Calculate it based on estimated duration.

\`\`\`typescript  
// src/lib/utils/credits.ts

export const calculateAudiobookCost \= (wordCount: number): number \=\> {  
  // Average reading speed: \~150 words per minute  
  const estimatedMinutes \= Math.ceil(wordCount / 150);  
    
  // Base cost \+ Variable cost per minute  
  // Example: 10 credits base \+ 5 credits per minute  
  const cost \= 10 \+ (estimatedMinutes \* 5);   
    
  return cost;  
};

// Example Usage in Worker:  
// Chapter has 1500 words \-\> \~10 mins \-\> Cost \= 10 \+ (10\*5) \= 60 Credits.  
\`\`\`

\#\#\#\# 2\. The "Insufficient Funds" Modal  
When the user clicks "Generate Audiobook" in the UI:

1\.  \*\*Frontend:\*\* Calls an API endpoint \`/api/books/\[id\]/estimate-audio\`.  
2\.  \*\*Backend:\*\* Returns \`{ estimatedCost: 60, userCredits: 10 }\`.  
3\.  \*\*Frontend Logic:\*\*  
    \*   If \`userCredits \>= estimatedCost\`: Show "Generate" button.  
    \*   If \`userCredits \< estimatedCost\`: Show "Upgrade Needed" card with two buttons:  
        \*   🔵 \*\*"Buy Credit Pack"\*\* (Opens Stripe Checkout for A-La-Carte)  
        \*   🟣 \*\*"Upgrade Plan"\*\* (Opens Stripe Portal to change subscription)

\#\#\#\# 3\. Update Your Database Schema (Optional but Recommended)  
Add a flag to track if a specific asset was generated via "Subscription Credits" or "One-Off Purchase" (good for analytics later, though not strictly required for V1).

\`\`\`sql  
\-- Add to media\_assets table  
alter table media\_assets add column payment\_source text check (payment\_source in ('subscription\_allowance', 'ala\_carte\_topup')) default 'subscription\_allowance';  
\`\`\`
